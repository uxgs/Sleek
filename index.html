    How Sleek works

    • It saves as you go. Decks live in this browser, on this device. No account, nothing to install, and it works offline once it has opened.
    • Keep a copy, or move a deck. Download its .sleek file from a deck’s ⋯ menu here, or Share in the deck. Open it on any device with Open a file…, or drop it on this page.
    • Link a folder (Chrome and Edge), and every deck keeps a .sleek copy there as you work.
    • Send it as a web page. Publish a deck as one page anyone can open in a browser, with nothing to install.
    • Present. Present opens a presenter window with your notes, the next slide and a timer, while this one shows the room the slide.
    • Install it. Add Sleek from your browser’s menu (Install, or Add to Home Screen) and it opens like an app.
