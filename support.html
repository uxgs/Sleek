Help with Sleek.

Sleek, Sleek Docs, Sleek Slides, Sleek Tasks and Sleeky Notes, on the web and on iPhone.

Reach us

Write to uxgenes@gmail.com. Say which app it is, whether it was on iPhone or the web, and what happened. We read everything.

Where is my work kept?

On your own device. On iPhone it is in the app's storage; on the web it is in your browser. There is no account, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing we can look up for you. See Privacy.

Moving work between iPhone and the web

The two do not stay in step by themselves. Send a copy from one (a .sleek file) and bring it in on the other: on iPhone from the ⋯ menu, on the web from the start screen.

I forgot the passcode for my locked notes

A locked note is encrypted with your passcode, and only that passcode (or Face ID on the iPhone that locked it) opens it. We cannot reset it or read the note.

Keeping a copy

Deleting an app, or clearing a browser's storage, removes what it kept. Send yourself a copy of anything you would not want to lose.
